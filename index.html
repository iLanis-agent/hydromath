🥬 HydroMath

Home hydroponics math that holds up. Reservoirs, nutrients, pH, light - numbers you can plan a grow on.

Reservoir sizing

Each crop drinks its own amount. Size the reservoir with a 25% buffer so roots never sit dry between top-offs.

Nutrient dosing & top-off

Dose by the bottle's rate on the full reservoir. Between full changes, top off with plain pH-adjusted water - plants drink water faster than they eat.

PPM & pH check

PPM by crop and stage, pH in the 5.5-6.5 window where nutrients stay available.

Light hours

DLI (daily light integral) is the honest light measure: PPFD x hours x 0.0036. Get hours for your crop's target, and a verdict on your current setup.
